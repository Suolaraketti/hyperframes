## Voice & tone (applies to ALL copy, incl. video scripts/captions)

- Casual, direct, human-to-human but professional. Write like a real person talking.
- **No em dashes, ever.** No corporate jargon. No AI-sounding phrases. No corporate polish.
- Short sentences. No slang (no beast/bro/sigma/lit/fire/crush it/hustle). No emojis/hashtags.
- Lead with the pain, not the product. Make the prospect feel seen.
- One primary pain per message. Peer-level (VP-to-VP), not vendor-to-prospect.
- Note: Dennis's personal LinkedIn must NEVER hint at Dialfyne/a side business — senior-AE
  observations only. Amber's LinkedIn leads with the Roleplay/sales-team side.
